Maintenance questions

How long is Aion 2 maintenance today?

Use the dated announcement and planned duration above. Previous windows differ; an old notice does not establish today's downtime. Check the official notice for extensions and completion.

Are maintenance and weekly reset the same?

No. Maintenance interrupts service; a reset refreshes activity allowances. See the daily and weekly reset timer for separately sourced reset rules.

Where are the latest patch notes?

Read the official patch notes. Update details may be published separately from maintenance times.

Is maintenance always at the same time?

Use a dated notice for each maintenance. Pacific and European daylight-saving changes occur on different dates, and temporary maintenance can be announced separately.
